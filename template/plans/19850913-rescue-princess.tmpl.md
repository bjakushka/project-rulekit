# Rescue Princess Peach

Status: active

## Goal

Mario and Luigi rescue Princess Peach from Bowser and return her safely to the
Mushroom Kingdom. The work is complete when the brothers reach the final
castle, defeat Bowser, and confirm that the princess is safe.

## Context

Source: independent plan; no backlog item

- Bowser holds the princess in the final castle
- The route crosses eight worlds with different obstacles
- Both brothers can continue the mission if the other loses a life

## Scope

In scope:

- Reaching the final castle
- Rescuing the princess
- Returning everyone safely

Out of scope:

- Rebuilding damaged castles
- Planning the victory celebration

## Approach

Travel together and clear one world at a time. Mario leads the direct route,
while Luigi checks alternate paths and keeps spare supplies.

## Steps

### 1. Prepare for the journey

- [ ] Collect enough power-ups for both brothers
- [ ] Confirm the route to World 1

### 2. Clear the route to the final castle

Cross the worlds in order so no blocked route is mistaken for completed work.
At the end of each world, confirm that the rescued resident is not the princess
and record the next castle. If the main route is blocked, Luigi checks an
alternate path before the brothers spend another life on the same obstacle.

Affected areas:

- Mushroom Kingdom roads and underground passages
- Castles in Worlds 1 through 8

- [ ] Clear each world and record the next destination
- [ ] Reach the entrance to the final castle with at least one brother active

### 3. Rescue the princess

- [ ] Defeat Bowser in the final castle
- [ ] Confirm that Princess Peach is safe
- [ ] Return to the Mushroom Kingdom

## Completion

- [ ] The princess and both brothers are safely home
- [ ] No required world or castle remains unresolved

## Follow-up

- Repair damaged routes and castles
- Plan the victory celebration
